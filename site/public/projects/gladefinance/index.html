01 / CONTEXT

Finance that fits the working day.

A business needs to understand available funds, make payments and return to a reliable transaction record. These connected tasks put clarity, authentication and dependable service integration at the centre of the mobile experience.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My part in the product.

I led mobile application development at GladeFinance, working across Flutter and native integrations. My scope included wallet and payment functionality, authentication, financial-service APIs, and release delivery.

03 / ENGINEERING

Financial workflows meet mobile reality.

I worked on mobile integrations with Node.js-backed services, maintained native modules, and connected analytics and crash reporting. Fastlane and GitLab pipelines supported the release process. The engineering focus was a coherent path from authentication through a financial operation, with clear handling of errors and application state.
