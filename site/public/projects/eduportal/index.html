Eduportal.

School, a little closer.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Platforms Innovations · Mobile development

PRODUCT FOCUS

Education workflows and mobile access

01 / CONTEXT

The school portal, in a student’s hands.

Eduportal brings results, learning content and school updates into one mobile place. School sign-in gives students access to the right information. The product archive also shows onboarding for assignments and checking results.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

Mobile education at Platforms Innovations.

My Platforms Innovations work involved cross-platform education functionality, backend integration and local persistence. Eduportal and Schoolgate are presented as separate product experiences within that education work.

03 / ENGINEERING CONTEXT

School data, made usable on mobile.

School identity, sign-in and server-supplied results need a consistent mobile presentation. The wider education work used React Native, REST integration and SQLite, with attention to local state and synchronization.
