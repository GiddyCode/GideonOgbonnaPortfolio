Superpow!.

Human connection. On screen.

MY ROLE

Mobile Application Developer · Demz Analytics · 2020–2021

TECHNOLOGY / FOCUS

React Native · Flutter · Firebase · WebRTC

01 / CONTEXT

Finding a therapist is personal.

Superpow centred on preferences, language and cultural fit, with video sessions bringing the conversation into a mobile experience. The aim was to make finding and meeting a compatible therapist easier; treatment outcomes are not claimed here.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My part in the product.

I contributed to Superpow through the Demz Analytics team, which developed the app for Superpow. This was collaborative client work, spanning mobile functionality and real-time communication.

03 / ENGINEERING

From an appointment to a conversation.

The work included Firebase-backed messaging and WebRTC video integration, together with mobile API integration, debugging and performance work. The experience brought discovery and communication into the same product.

03B / PRODUCT JOURNEY

How the experience connects.

  1. Share preferencesSet the context for finding a therapist.
  2. Choose a sessionReview options and arrange an appointment.
  3. Meet remotelyConnect through the product’s video experience.
