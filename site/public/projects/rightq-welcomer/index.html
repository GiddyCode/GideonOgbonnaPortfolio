RightQ Welcomer.

A warmer welcome. A clearer queue.

PROJECT CONTEXT

RightCom · Mobile application development

PRODUCT FOCUS

Mobile workflows for service teams

01 / CONTEXT

Turn an arrival into a service request.

Welcomer gives front-desk teams a practical way to register visitors and direct them to the right service. Staff can select an area, create tickets and monitor customer flow from a mobile device. It is the assisted check-in side of the RightQ experience.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My part in the RightCom team.

My RightCom work covered mobile customer-service workflows, check-in, queue monitoring and device/backend integration. Welcomer sits within that team-built service environment.

03 / ENGINEERING CONTEXT

Connected actions, consistent queue state.

A service choice needs to become a ticket that the wider queue platform understands. The engineering context is the relationship between Android device actions, backend data and clear operator feedback. My wider RightCom work also included device configuration and QA.
