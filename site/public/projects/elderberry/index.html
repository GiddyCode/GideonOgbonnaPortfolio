Elderberry.

Care, with connection.

MY ROLE

Senior Mobile Engineer · 2023–2025

TECHNOLOGY / FOCUS

Flutter · Swift · Kotlin · Firebase · REST

01 / CONTEXT

Care has two sides to coordinate.

Families need to find home-care support that fits, while providers need a clear view of when they are needed. Elderberry brings those needs together through profiles, availability and bookings. The mobile experience needs to keep changing care schedules understandable.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My part in the product.

As a Senior Mobile Engineer, I worked on caregiver-facing features across Flutter and native iOS and Android. My work covered scheduling, bookings, reporting and live tracking, alongside authentication and API integration.

03 / ENGINEERING

Keeping the experience in step.

I integrated REST services and Firebase/Firestore synchronization, using modular architecture and deliberate error handling. Testing, profiling, debugging and code review supported maintainability as the product grew. I collaborated with Product, Design, Backend and QA across delivery.

03B / PRODUCT JOURNEY

How the experience connects.

  1. Find supportEnter the care-coordination experience.
  2. Arrange a bookingConnect availability with the support needed.
  3. Manage the dayKeep provider schedules and service updates accessible.
