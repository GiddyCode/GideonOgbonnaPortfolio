RightQ Kiosk.

A better way to wait.

MY ROLE

Mobile Application Developer · RightCom · 2017–2020

TECHNOLOGY / FOCUS

Android · Java · Kotlin · Backend integration

01 / CONTEXT

An obvious first step on arrival.

The kiosk is the customer-facing, self-service side of RightQ. Visitors choose a service and receive a ticket, while the wider platform coordinates the queue. RightQ Welcomer supports staff-assisted arrivals; this interface serves the person standing at the kiosk.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

My part in the product.

At RightCom, I contributed mobile and kiosk functionality for customer-service operations, including check-in, real-time queue monitoring and NFC integrations.

03 / ENGINEERING

Software at the point of service.

The work connected device interfaces with backend services and the wider queue workflow. I collaborated with QA on defects, device configurations and release reliability, alongside ongoing modernization and maintenance.

03B / PRODUCT JOURNEY

How the experience connects.

  2. Confirm the requestProvide the information needed to join the queue.
